About

Hi, I’m Jonathan, founder of Studio of Worlds. I’m a storyteller and creative director working with AI as a production medium. I create cinematic shorts, music videos, original characters, and story worlds across fantasy, science fiction, history, and animation — developing ideas from concept through image, motion, sound, and final edit. AI has opened a new kind of independent production: work that once required large teams and budgets can now be created by small studios and individual artists with strong ideas, taste, and direction. That possibility is what drives Studio of Worlds. My influences range from Roger Deakins and Ridley Scott to David Lean and J.R.R. Tolkien, but the goal is always the same: create original work with its own identity. Ultimately, I’m building intellectual property — characters, worlds, stories, music, and visual concepts designed to grow beyond a single piece of content. My goal is simple: tell good stories, keep learning, share what I discover, and bring a little more wonder into the world. AI & Production Pipeline: Midjourney, Kling AI, Flora AI, Nano Banana Pro, Suno, ElevenLabs, Seedance 2.5, Meshy AI, DaVinci Resolve, Photoshop, After Effects.
