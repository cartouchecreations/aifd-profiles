About

AI filmmaker and commercial director in Portugal creating cinematic AI commercials, product videos, UGC ads and narrative content. Roni Beraha brings 20+ years of professional film-production experience as a focus puller and camera operator, with production experience on projects involving Ridley Scott, Netflix, and Amazon. Today, he combines traditional cinematography and commercial filmmaking expertise with AI production workflows to develop and execute films from concept through final delivery. Services include AI video generation, AI commercial production, AI storytelling and creative direction, AI previsualization, AI character-consistent video, AI motion and animation, and AI editing/post-production. Roni works with tools including Veo, Kling, Seedance, Higgsfield, NanoBanana, ElevenLabs, CapCut and DaVinci Resolve. He is particularly focused on cinematic short-form content, UGC advertising, fashion and commercial storytelling, with remote production available for clients worldwide. For brands, agencies, production companies and creators looking for professionally directed AI video rather than generic AI-generated content, contact Roni to discuss your project, concept, or campaign.
