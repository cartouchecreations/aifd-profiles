About

Animation has always been the most expensive, most gatekept form of filmmaking. $50M budgets. 500-person teams. 5-year timelines. Studio backing or nothing. We built Syscroft Studio to end that. We create feature-quality animated films, shorts, features, brand films, episodic series, using AI-assisted production without compromising on craft. The result: cinema that looks like it cost $50M, delivered in weeks, at budgets independent creators can actually raise. This is how we work: Every Syscroft project starts the same way great films always have, with story, character, and vision. Human creative direction drives everything. AI is our production pipeline, not our creative director. We've produced award-winning festival shorts in 5 weeks. Brand films with 2.4M+ views in 3 weeks. Experimental narratives that traditional animation would have priced at $1M+, built by teams of 3. We work with independent filmmakers, brands, content creators, and IP owners who've been told animation is out of reach. It isn't anymore. If you have a story that deserves animation, let's talk.
