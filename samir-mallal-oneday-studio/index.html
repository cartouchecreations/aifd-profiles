About

Samir Mallal Co-Founder, CEO & Director | OneDay Studio Location: London, United Kingdom (Canadian, born Halifax, Nova Scotia) About Samir Mallal is a Canadian filmmaker and the Co-Founder and CEO of OneDay Studio — a director-led, global AI entertainment studio whose mission is to tell stories at the speed of culture using the latest generative AI tools. With a career spanning 15+ years across commercials, documentary, virtual reality, and now AI-hybrid cinema, Samir is one of the most credentialed filmmaker-founders in the AI production space. His pre-AI career alone speaks for itself: a Cannes Gold Lion , a Sundance Grand Jury Prize nomination , a Canadian Screen Award nomination , and the distinction of having his commercial Arctic Sun named by TIFF as the best commercial in Canadian cinema history . He was named Best Young Director at Cannes Lions 2010 and selected for the Saatchi & Saatchi New Directors' Showcase . At OneDay, Samir and co-founder Bouha Kazmi have built a custom AI filmmaking pipeline — blending live performance, cinematography, and AI-rendered environments — that Variety described as "Mission: Impossible for the News" and The New York Times said "rivals Hollywood productions."
