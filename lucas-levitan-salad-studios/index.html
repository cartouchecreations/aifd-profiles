About

Lucas Levitan is a Madrid-based AI filmmaker, illustrator, and creative director specializing in AI animation, AI short film, and brand storytelling. Brazilian-born with over 175,000 Instagram followers, he is internationally recognized for Photo Invasion — his illustration project where he adds original hand-drawn characters to other people's photographs to create unexpected one-frame stories. Previously Creative Director at Meta and Mother agency, he now leads Levitan Line, his independent creative studio. His AI short film Little Mes (4:19), co-created with Fabáin Jiménez, was selected as one of 10 finalists from 2,181 global submissions at Runway's 4th Annual International AI Festival (AIF 2026). The film explores the question: "Who would you have become if you had taken a different path?" Specialties: AI animation · Illustration · Creative direction · AI short film · Brand storytelling Tools: Runway · Google Veo Available for brand projects, AI short films, illustration, and creative direction.
