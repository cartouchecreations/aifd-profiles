About

AI filmmaker & two-time Cannes Gold Lion winner. Top 40 global AI Filmmakers. 68+ awards; features in development with Massive Studios. Sündermann (LCLS Studio) directs narrative film, brand campaigns, and music video work, and is available for narrative feature and short development, commercial and brand film direction, and music video direction. Recent honors include a Cannes Gold Lion (Victoria), a Grand Prix at the Festival of Media, a D&AD Pencil, and multiple CLIO and Effie golds, for campaigns including Corona, HP, and Aeroméxico. Collaborators include A$AP Rocky, Tilda Swinton, Ewan McGregor, Paul McCartney, Lady Gaga, and Sylvester Stallone. His long-form original series have crossed 100M+ combined views, and he is currently writing and directing narrative features in development with Massive Studios alongside Roger Avary and Reza Sixo Safai. His fine art photography has shown at Art Basel Miami and Paris Gallery Weekend. For narrative, brand, or music video projects that need festival-level craft, reach out below.
