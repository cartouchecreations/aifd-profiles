About

One-Sentence Summary Videostate is an AI filmmaking studio specializing in cinematic teaser trailers, brand storytelling films, and original film IP development for studios, brands, and creators. Business Description Videostate is an AI filmmaking studio founded by filmmaker and creative director Jan-Willem Blom in the Netherlands. The studio produces cinematic videos, teaser trailers, and narrative proof-of-concept films using generative AI as part of the filmmaking process. The work focuses on cinematic storytelling and worldbuilding. Videostate develops characters, environments, and mythology that allow stories to expand beyond a single video into larger narrative worlds. Projects range from branded storytelling films and marketing campaigns to original film concepts and experimental narrative formats exploring the future of cinema with AI. Alongside production work, Videostate also shares knowledge through workshops, mentoring, and educational programs that help filmmakers, creative studios, and organizations understand how AI can be used within professional storytelling workflows.
