About

Andrew Salter is a Bristol-based filmmaker, video editor, and motion designer known for his pioneering work in AI-generated cinema. His AI short film Jailbird won 2nd place at the 2025 Runway AI Film Festival, one of the most competitive AI film events globally with thousands of submissions. The film invites viewers to experience the world through the eyes of a chicken being sent to a human prison, told entirely through AI-generated visuals created using tools such as Runway. Jailbird was also showcased at the invideo India AI Film Festival 2026 in New Delhi. Andrew has a background in trailer and sizzle reel editing for major broadcasters, having worked across genres including natural history, premium factual, and documentary. He studied Zoology at university and brings a unique scientific curiosity to his storytelling. He is currently working at Passion Pictures as a Development Assistant Producer and Sizzle Reel Editor.
