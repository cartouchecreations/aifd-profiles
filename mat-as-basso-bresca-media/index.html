About

Screenwriter (Paco's Men) and BBC Talent Award winner making AI microdramas, commercials and branded series for brands. Barcelona, works worldwide. I wrote for Paco's Men, the Antena 3 police series co-created by Álex Pina. My work has aired on the BBC, Channel 4, Al Jazeera and Arte, and I have made brand films in the Gulf for stc, Experience Abu Dhabi, Visit Qatar and Qatar Foundation. At Bresca Media I write and direct series built around a brand: one to two minute episodes for the phone, with characters people follow and a story that carries the product. We produce with AI, live action or both, depending on budget and audience. What you can hire me for Branded microdrama series, starting with a ten-episode pilot 30-second AI commercials, with 15 and 6-second cutdowns Trailers and proof-of-concept scenes for writers and producers Scripts, series bibles and creative direction for AI productions I deliver in English and Spanish for clients in Europe, the Gulf and the US. Tools: Seedance, Kling, Nano Banana, Midjourney, Magnific, ElevenLabs. FAQ What is a branded microdrama? A series of one to two minute vertical episodes with recurring characters. The brand sits inside the story, and every episode links to it. Who is a good fit? Brands and agencies that want an audience coming back every week, and writers or producers who need to show a story before it is funded. Send a brief to mbasso@brescamedia.com or see the slate at brescamedia.com/drama. Reel: vimeo.com/mbasso
