About

Gavin Carlton, AccompliceFX. AI filmmaker and 20 year VFX veteran making cinematic AI ads for DTC, beauty and med spa brands. Los Angeles, remote. I spent two decades in Hollywood post production, on shows and specials including Mr. Robot, Day Shift and The Weeknd: Live at SoFi Stadium, before building an AI pipeline on top of that craft. That combination is the whole point. Most AI video still looks like AI video. Mine is composited, graded and finished in After Effects the way a broadcast spot is, so generated footage sits next to your real product photography without falling apart. Hire me for: cinematic AI video ads for DTC and ecommerce brands, AI product demo videos, 15 second vertical spots for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, hybrid AI plus live action compositing, and long form AI documentary work. Recent work: an 11 location cinematic chair commercial for Ozzi Gear composited with real product shots; a multi round football commercial composite for Natrol delivered in log; vertical med spa campaigns for Smooth and Skinny, shot on location by me and rebuilt in Seedance with ElevenLabs voiceover; product ads for MODA MODA Zero Gray and OVENA Health. On my Generative History channel I write and direct AI historical documentaries, including the 23 minute film The Sultana Disaster of 1865. Best Visual Effects, Feature Film, 2017. Send me your product and the platform it is running on and I will come back with a concept and a quote.
