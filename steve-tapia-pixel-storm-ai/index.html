About

AI filmmaker & creative director | Pixel Storm Media | Cinematic AI video for real estate & brands | Remote / Worldwide Steve Tapia is the founder of Pixel Storm Media, a full-service AI creative agency producing cinematic video content for real estate developers, agents, property dealers, and brand founders who need polished results without traditional production timelines or costs. Every project is fully AI-integrated from concept to delivery — scripts generated with LLMs, storyboarding in Milanote, visuals rendered in Kling 3.0 and NanoBanana Pro, voiceovers built in ElevenLabs, scores composed in Suno, and final edit and sound design completed in DaVinci Resolve. Start to finish in days, not weeks. Core services: AI real estate flythroughs and property walkthroughs, AI short-form and UGC video ads, AI long-form brand videos, hybrid AI video podcasts, and done-for-you monthly content retainers. One recent result: a manufactured home dealer in California had a listing sit on the market for a full year. Within 24 hours of posting an AI video produced by Pixel Storm Media, it received the highest view count the listing had ever seen. If you need cinematic AI video that makes properties feel lived-in and brands feel premium — let's talk. Pricing varies and monthly retainers available with a 3-month minimum.
