Paul Trillo Multi-Disciplinary Artist, Writer & Director | Co-Founder, Asteria Films About Paul Trillo is a multi-disciplinary artist, writer, and director whose work sits at the intersection of experimental filmmaking, cutting-edge technology, and conceptual art. Known for challenging the boundaries of what's possible on screen, Paul's career spans practical effects, aerial choreography, bullet-time innovation, and AI-hybrid production — all driven by an obsessive commitment to continuous reinvention. He is a co-founder and partner at Asteria Films , an innovation-led production company with a mission to bring an ethical approach to AI in the film industry.
