About

Michael Cole is an AI filmmaker specializing in AI-generated films, commercials, and motion-driven visual storytelling for brands, artists, agencies, and arts organizations. AI Filmmaker Description Michael Cole is an AI filmmaker, motion graphics designer, and visual storyteller creating AI-generated films, commercials, experimental shorts, and music-driven visuals. With a background in broadcast design, animation, contemporary performance, and visual art, he brings a strong sense of composition, rhythm, storytelling, and editorial polish to AI filmmaking for brands, agencies, artists, and arts organizations. His process combines concept development, writing, image generation, image-to-video workflows, editing, compositing, sound, and finishing across a wide range of generative tools. Depending on the project, he develops visuals from prompts, style frames, and storyboards, then builds the final piece through iterative testing, curation, motion design judgment, and post-production refinement. Michael’s work often blends cinematic atmosphere, transformation, fashion, architecture, surrealism, movement, and emotional tone. He is especially interested in using AI to create visually rich films that feel designed rather than generic, and in helping clients and collaborators discover the right AI workflow for a specific creative goal.
