About

One-Sentence Summary Zoubeir Jlassi is an award-winning Tunisian AI filmmaker and creative director who won the world's first $1,000,000 AI Film Award at the 2026 1 Billion Followers Summit in Dubai for his film Lily . AI Filmmaker Description Zoubeir Jlassi is a Tunisian filmmaker, art director, and visual storyteller with over 15 years of experience in design and moving image. CEO and Creative Director of Baya & Lily Studios, he is widely recognized as one of the world's most accomplished AI filmmakers after winning the inaugural $1 million AI Film Award — the largest AI film prize ever awarded — at the 2026 1 Billion Followers Summit in Dubai, competing against 3,500 submissions from 116 countries. His award-winning short film Lily , created using Google Veo 3, Google Gemini, and Nano Banana Pro, explores solitude and emotion through a distinctive AI Claymation style, and was personally inspired by his daughter. Prior to Lily , his film A Breath Apart earned 10 international awards across festivals in the US, Brazil, Nigeria, Algeria, and Turkey. Jlassi is now in pre-production on his first feature-length AI film expanding the Lily universe.
