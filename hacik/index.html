About

"I am not interested in using AI to generate images. I am interested in using AI to generate ideas worth turning into films." About me I am a French filmmaker, screenwriter and AI Director, exploring the intersection between storytelling, advertising concepts and the creative possibilities of generative AI. Coming from the world of audiovisual production and communication, I have always been driven by strong ideas, unique visual worlds and stories capable of creating an emotional impact. Today, I use AI as a new directing tool, combined with my experience in writing, filmmaking and post-production. My approach is to start with a concept, an intention or a story, and then explore how AI-powered workflows can help bring ambitious visions to life — whether for films, commercials or narrative content. I don’t see AI as the final destination, but as a new creative language serving an idea, an emotion and a directorial vision. What I Bring A strong concept is always the starting point. I enjoy creating ideas that surprise, provoke emotion or spark conversation. Drawing from both cinematic storytelling and advertising culture, I aim to build films that are remembered for their creative proposition rather than the technology behind them. My work combines writing, directing, AI generation and post-production to deliver complete visual experiences from concept to final film.
